My work operates at the intersection of **psychological medicine**, **computational neuroimaging**, and **behavioural data analysis**. I am interested in moving beyond reductive models of mental health by using multimodal data and phenomenological experience to improve our understanding of mental health conditions. My work as a Research Fellow at the **University of Nottingham** is currently focused on focused ultrasound stimulation and diffusion MRI methods development.

I am the developer and maintainer of [NFACT](https://nfact.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).

Previously, I completed my PhD in Psychological Medicine at **King’s College London**, investigating how socio-emotional processing, cognitive functions, and autistic traits relate to underlying neurobiology in eating disorders (specifically Anorexia Nervosa) through resting-state fMRI cyclic connectivity, diffusion MRI, and machine learning.
