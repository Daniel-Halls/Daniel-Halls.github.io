My interest is in moving beyond reductive models of mental health by using multimodal data, phenomenological experience, philosophical theories and computational methods. By seeing mental health as a domain of psychological distress and moving beyond pathological views of the brain I want to understand the complexity of mental health.

My post doctoral work at the Precision Imaging department University of Nottingham, has focused on improving diffusion MRI for use in individuals with mental health disorders and the biological effects of focused ultrasound. Previously, I completed my PhD in Psychological Medicine at **King’s College London**, investigating how socio-emotional processing, cognitive functions, and autistic traits relate to underlying neurobiology in eating disorders (specifically Anorexia Nervosa).

I am the developer and maintainer of [NFACT](https://nfact.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) and [NEURI](https://github.com/Daniel-Halls/neuri).
